Seojunyang (owner: Junhyung Seo) takes care of the personal information of everyone who uses 1 Day 1 Diary. This policy explains what we receive, why, and how we protect it.

*This is an English translation of the Korean policy. If the two differ, the [Korean version](/1day1diary/privacy/) prevails.*

> **At a glance**
> 📒 Your entries, photos, moods, and places stay on your device and your own Google Drive. The developer can't see them.
> 🙅 There's no sign-up, and no server that collects your entries.
> 📊 The developer only receives usage statistics and crash reports, which never include what you write.
> 📢 The free version shows ads, so your advertising ID is shared with the ad provider.

---

## 1. Information the developer never receives

Your entries, photos, moods, weather, places, and lock passcode are never sent to the developer, and are never included in usage statistics, crash reports, or ads.

- **Your device:** Entries and photos are stored in space that only this app can access.
- **Google Drive:** If you turn on backup, they are stored in your own Google Drive. The app can only access the backup files it created, and the developer can't see your Drive.
- **[Use my location]:** Only when you tap this button, your location is sent to the operating system to turn it into an address. Coordinates are not saved; only the place name stays in your entry.

## 2. Information the developer receives

| Purpose | Kept for | What we receive |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Improving the app (usage statistics) | Up to 14 months | Screens opened, buttons tapped, item counts (as ranges, not exact values), device model, OS, app version, app install ID |
| Fixing errors (crash reports) | 90 days | Time and code location of the error, device model, OS, app version, app install ID |
| Showing ads (free version) | Per the ad provider's policy | Advertising ID, approximate region estimated from IP address, ad impression and click records |
| Verifying and restoring purchases | 5 years after the subscription ends | Product purchased, purchase time, subscription status, anonymous buyer ID |
| Answering inquiries | 3 years after the reply | Email address, inquiry content, app version, OS, device model |

Payment details such as card numbers are never sent to the developer. Payments are handled by the store you downloaded the app from (Google Play or the App Store).

### When you ask us to repair a damaged diary

If an old diary was only partly moved over, we receive your old diary file only when you choose [Attach and contact us] in the app yourself.

- **What we receive:** Only the one old diary file. Photos, the lock passcode, and settings are not included. The app shows you what's inside before you send it.
- **Where we receive it:** Only at our contact email (sjunh812@naver.com).
- **Purpose:** Used only to repair your diary. We don't read it for any other purpose or move it elsewhere.
- **Deletion:** After we send the repaired file back, we immediately delete the file we received and the file we made from our mailbox and trash. If you ask, we delete it right away, even before the repair. The email itself is kept as described under "Answering inquiries" above.

## 3. Information collected automatically, and how to opt out

Usage statistics, crash reports, and ads use the app install ID and advertising ID. If you don't want this, you can opt out as follows.

- **Android:** Reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings > Privacy > Ads.
- **iPhone and iPad:** Before showing ads for the first time, we ask once whether you allow tracking. If you don't, the advertising ID isn't used. You can change this anytime in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
- **Personalized ads:** If you delete your advertising ID or don't allow tracking, interest-based ads won't be shown. Non-personalized ads are still shown.
- **Using the app without ads:** If you buy Remove Ads or Plus, the app doesn't load ads.
- **Stopping collection:** Deleting the app stops all collection.

Ads are never chosen based on what you write.

### If you're in the European Economic Area (EEA), the UK, or Switzerland

- **Ad consent:** Before showing ads for the first time, we ask through Google's consent form whether you agree to personalized ads and to storing ad information. You can use every feature of the app even if you don't agree, and ads are shown without personalized information.
- **Changing your consent:** You can change or withdraw your consent anytime in the app's Settings > Ad privacy settings.
- **Legal basis:** Personalized ads and storing ad information are based on your consent. Usage statistics and crash reports are based on our legitimate interest in running the app reliably, and you can opt out as described in section 3. Purchase verification is based on performing our contract with you.
- **Your rights:** In addition to the rights in section 6, you can ask for data portability and file a complaint with the data protection authority of the country you live in.

## 5. Deleting personal information

Information is deleted without delay once its retention period ends, and electronic files are deleted so they can't be recovered. You can also delete things yourself:

- **Entries on your device:** Empty the app's Trash or delete the app. Entries in Trash are deleted automatically after 30 days.
- **Drive backups:** Delete them in the app's backup settings or in Google Drive.
- **Disconnecting Drive:** Disconnect in the app's backup settings or in your Google Account's security settings.

## 6. Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, delete, or stop the processing of your personal information at any time. Email us at the address below and we'll handle it without delay. Because there's no sign-up, the developer can't verify who you are for some information; you can remove that information by deleting the app or using the methods in section 3.

## 7. How we protect personal information

- We don't keep your entries or photos on any developer server.
- All communication is encrypted with HTTPS.
- Google Drive access is limited to files the app created.
- You can lock the app with a passcode or biometrics.
- Only the owner handles personal information.
